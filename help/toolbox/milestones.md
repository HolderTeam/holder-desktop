# Milestones

Use Milestones to see a project's important dates alongside the cards created or updated each day.

Days with activity are marked in the calendar. Select a day to see its milestones, newly created cards, and updated cards. Card titles open the corresponding card.

Choose **Add milestone** to attach a deadline, appointment, event, or another dated marker to a card. Use **Upcoming** for a chronological view of future milestones, and **Today** to return to the current date.
